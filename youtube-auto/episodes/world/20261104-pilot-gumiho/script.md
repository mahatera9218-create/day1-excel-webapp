# The Bride With Nine Shadows | A Korean Ghost Tale (Gumiho)

> 소재: The Fox Spirit Bride (Korea - Gumiho)

**설명**

A lonely scholar is taken in by a beautiful stranger on a fog-bound mountain road. But her shadow does not move the way a woman's should. A retelling of the Korean tale of the Gumiho, the nine-tailed fox.

Source: traditional Korean folklore (구미호 설화), as commonly retold.
