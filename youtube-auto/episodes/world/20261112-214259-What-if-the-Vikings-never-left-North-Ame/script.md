> 소재: What if the Vikings never left North America (Vinland / 1000 AD)

**설명**

Everyone learns that Columbus found America in 1492. But Viking sailors actually landed there around the year 1000, half a thousand years earlier, and then walked away. What if they had stayed? A bold what-if about the Norse in North America.

A what-if scenario: real Norse history up to about 1000 AD, then imagination. Sources: the Norse sagas and the L'Anse aux Meadows archaeological site in Newfoundland.
