# The Town That Couldn't Stop Dancing | The Dancing Plague of 1518

> 소재: The Dancing Plague of 1518 (Strasbourg)

**설명**

In the summer of 1518, a woman in Strasbourg began to dance in the street — and within weeks, hundreds had joined her, unable to stop. It really happened, and historians still can't fully explain it.

Sources: contemporary Strasbourg chronicles and physician records; modern analyses by historian John Waller and others. Details such as the death toll are debated.
