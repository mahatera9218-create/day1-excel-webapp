> 소재: The Walking Statues of Rapa Nui (Easter Island / Archaeology)

**설명**

For hundreds of years, the people of Rapa Nui said their giant stone statues 'walked' to the coast. Outsiders were sure that was just a story. Then a small team picked up some ropes and tested it. This is the true story of Easter Island's walking statues.

100% true history, told like a drama. Sources: Lipo & Hunt field experiments and 2025 Journal of Archaeological Science study; Rapa Nui oral tradition.
