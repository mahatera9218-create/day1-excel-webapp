# The Queen Who Seduced Two Roman Titans | Cleopatra & Mark Antony

> 소재: Cleopatra and Mark Antony (Egypt / Rome)

**설명**

She was the last queen of Egypt — and she bent two of the most powerful men in Rome to her will. The forbidden love of Cleopatra and Mark Antony scandalized an empire and helped bring down a dynasty.

Sources: Plutarch's Lives and standard histories of the late Roman Republic. Some vivid details (the carpet, the asp) are traditional accounts and are flagged as such.
