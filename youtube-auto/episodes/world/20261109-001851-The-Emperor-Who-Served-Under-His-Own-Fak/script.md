# This Emperor Invented a Fake Man — Then Worked For Him | Ming China

> 소재: The Ming emperor who invented a fake general named Zhu Shou and served under his own made-up name (China / early 1500s)

**설명**

In the early 1500s, one of China's emperors got bored of being emperor. So he invented a general who did not exist, gave that fake man a name and a rank, and then went to serve under him. His own officials had to salute a person who was really just their emperor in a costume. This is one of the strangest true stories in all of Chinese history.

100% true, kept careful where the records are unsure. Sources: Ming history records on the Zhengde Emperor (Zhu Houzhao); the alter ego Zhu Shou, from 1517.
