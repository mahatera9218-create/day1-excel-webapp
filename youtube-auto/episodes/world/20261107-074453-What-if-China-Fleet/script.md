# What If China Never Stopped Sailing? | The Biggest Fleet in History

> 소재: What if China's giant treasure fleet never stopped sailing? (China / the age of exploration)

**설명**

China once had the biggest ships on Earth — and sailed them all the way to Africa, long before Columbus. Then they stopped, and let the fleet rot. What if they never had? A grounded what-if, in plain English.

Real history: Admiral Zheng He's seven voyages (1405-1433) under the Ming emperor, and China's later turn away from the sea. The alternate future is speculation. Sources: standard histories of Zheng He's treasure-fleet voyages.
