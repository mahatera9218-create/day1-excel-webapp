# Soldiers Saw This City and Thought They Were Dreaming | Mexico, 1519

> 소재: The night Spanish soldiers first saw Tenochtitlan and could not believe it was real (Mexico / 1519)

**설명**

In November 1519, a few hundred Spanish soldiers walked over a lake and saw something they could not explain. A city bigger than any in Europe, floating on the water, white towers shining in the sun. Some of them thought they were dreaming. This is that first look, told as if you are standing right there.

100% true history, from the eyewitness who wrote it down. Sources: memoir of Bernal Diaz del Castillo; the Spanish entry into Tenochtitlan, 8 November 1519.
