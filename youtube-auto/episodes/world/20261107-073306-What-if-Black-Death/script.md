# What If the Black Death Never Happened? | The Plague That Built the Modern World

> 소재: What if the Black Death never happened? (Europe / the modern world)

**설명**

What if the deadliest pandemic in history had never struck Europe? You'd expect a golden age — but the Black Death may have been the shock that ended serfdom and lit the fuse of the modern world. A grounded what-if scenario.

Factual spine: the Black Death (c.1347-1351), the post-plague labor shortage and wage rises, the Statute of Labourers (1351), and the Peasants' Revolt (1381). Everything after the branch point is informed speculation. Sources: standard historical accounts (incl. Wikipedia: Black Death; Statute of Labourers 1351).
