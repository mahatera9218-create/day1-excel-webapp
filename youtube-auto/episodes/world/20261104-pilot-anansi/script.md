# How Anansi the Spider Won Every Story in the World | West African Folklore

> 소재: Anansi and the Sky God's Stories (West Africa)

**설명**

Long ago, every story on Earth belonged to the Sky God — until one small spider dared to bargain for them all. A retelling of the Akan tale of Anansi.

Source: traditional Ashanti (Akan) folklore of West Africa, as commonly retold.
